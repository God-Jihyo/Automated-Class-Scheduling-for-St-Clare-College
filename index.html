The Automated Scheduling and Calendar Management System is a software solution designed to automatically generate and manage schedules for academic sections.

Learn more →

Automated Scheduling

The automated scheduling system is designed to make it easier for both students and teachers to view and follow their class schedules without confusion or manual coordination. The system automatically creates clear and organized schedules for students and teachers. It assigns subjects, time slots, and teachers while ensuring there are no conflicts or overlaps. Students can easily view their class schedules, while teachers receive balanced and properly assigned teaching loads. Overall, it simplifies scheduling, saves time, and improves organization for everyone.

Academic Calendar

The system provides an organized academic calendar that displays important dates such as class schedules, events, and deadlines. It allows students and teachers to easily view and keep track of their activities in one place. By centralizing all schedule-related information, the calendar helps users stay informed, avoid conflicts, and manage their time more effectively

Real-time Updates

The system provides real-time updates to ensure that any changes in schedules, rooms, or assignments are instantly reflected in the calendar. This allows students and teachers to always see the most current and accurate information. With real-time updates, users are immediately informed of changes, helping them avoid confusion, stay organized, and respond quickly to any schedule adjustments. .
